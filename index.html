My path into data started in Public Management, where I learned to read organizations as systems of people, rules and trade-offs, and to question what a number really represents before relying on it.

On a daily basis, I use Excel and Power BI to clean, structure, and visualize data into clear, reliable dashboards from tracking headcount and turnover to automating recurring routines and profit-sharing calculations.
